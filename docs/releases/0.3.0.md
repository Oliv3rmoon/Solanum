Solanum 0.3.0: background music. Put WAV, MP3 or OGG files in `/data/solanum/music` and they play behind the store,
in order or shuffled, and fade out as an app opens.

**Install:** unzip `PPSA76526.zip` and copy its `PPSA76526` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`, over
0.2.0 or new. It needs the HEN's payload loader (port 9021) for its install helper and launch payload. 0.2.0 updates
itself to it.

- **Your own music.** Copy WAV (16-bit PCM), MP3 or OGG Vorbis files, mono or stereo at any sample rate, into
  `/data/solanum/music`, a folder Solanum makes. They play one after another in the order of their names (`2.wav`
  before `10.wav`), and files you add meanwhile join in when the list comes round. Other kinds (FLAC, M4A, Opus)
  are not played.
- **Settings > Interface:** **Background music** (on, and silent until there are files), **Shuffle music** (every file
  once before any plays again) and **Music volume** (10 to 100 %, 50 % to start with). Interface volume stays the
  clicks' own.
- **It fades out when an app opens,** so the app starts in silence, and fades in when it starts or is switched on.
- **The clicks keep working** over the music: they are mixed into it.
- The new settings are in all ten languages.

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA76526.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Solanum`, or against `SHA256SUMS`.
