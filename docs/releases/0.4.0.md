Solanum 0.4.0: 154 apps. ProsperoStore's catalog's apps are in Solanum's catalog now, 98 more, and Solanum installs
them all: apps that publish only pre-releases, ports that need your own game files, and apps that share a title ID
with another.

**Install:** unzip `PPSA76526.zip` and copy its `PPSA76526` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`, over
0.3.0 or new. It needs the HEN's payload loader (port 9021) for its install helper and launch payload. 0.3.0 updates
itself to it.

- **103 more apps,** from ProsperoStore's catalog: decompiled and recompiled console games and engine ports that run
  your own game files, PC-game launchers on Wine, more emulators (RetroArch, PS5SX2, Snes9x, Genesis Plus GX, FBNeo,
  Arcade Gallery...), media and streaming clients, AI apps and tools. Six of its apps are left out by the catalog's
  rules (backup tools, a release with game data, and two releases with nothing to check) and five by choice.
- **Needs your own game files** marks a port that ships none: it runs the copy of a game you own, which you copy
  over as its README says.
- **No license** marks an app whose repository states none. Solanum installs the author's own release, as published.
- **Pre-release apps install.** An app that publishes only pre-releases (or other platforms' releases between its
  own) is offered its newest release with its file, and its page says **Pre-release**.
- **Two apps of one title ID.** vkQuake and Dulcamara share one, and a console holds one of them. Solanum shows which
  one is installed, and asks before installing the other in its place (Versions too); your saved data stays. When it
  can't tell which app is there, it says so and leaves it alone.
- **nightly, the community.** About shows the Nightshade apps' Discord server, `discord.gg/pSqgRU3YP9`, with a QR
  code your phone opens it with; Triangle shows the code larger. The first start shows it once on a card, and any
  button closes it for good.
- In all ten languages.

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA76526.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Solanum`, or against `SHA256SUMS`.
