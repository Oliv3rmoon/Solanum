Solanum 0.5.0, a test release: the apps' own icons in the store, and a new icon for Solanum.

**Install:** unzip `PPSA76526.zip` and copy its `PPSA76526` folder to `/data/homebrew/` with permissions `777`, over
0.4.0 or new. It needs the HEN's payload loader (port 9021) for its install helper and launch payload. As a
pre-release it isn't offered by Solanum's own update check: install it by hand.

- **App icons in the store.** An app the catalog gives an icon shows it on its tile, in every list and on its page,
  with the tile's rounded corners. Nightshade, Physalis, Datura, Mandrake, Dulcamara and Solanum have theirs; the
  other apps keep their letter tiles until the catalog lists their icons.
- **Fetched only when you see them,** from Solanum's catalog's repository, and shown only when the file's size and
  SHA-256 are the ones the catalog gives. Each one is kept on the console, so the icons show without the network on
  the next start. At most 16 icons are held in memory, about 4 MB.
- **A new icon for Solanum:** a nightshade flower whose cone is a download arrow into a tray.

Check the download with `gh attestation verify PPSA76526.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Solanum`, or against `SHA256SUMS`.
