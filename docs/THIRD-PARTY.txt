Source code: https://github.com/Oliv3rmoon/Solanum (the release's tag holds the exact source, with every
component below in kit/, helper/, third_party/, or pinned by its build script).

Solanum is built on Dulcamara (https://github.com/Oliv3rmoon/Dulcamara), whose frontend is Mandrake's (formerly
Nightshade Station, https://github.com/Oliv3rmoon/Mandrake), which is darkxex's SwanStationPS5
(https://github.com/darkxex/SwanStationPS5, GPL-3.0-or-later) on BlackBearReloaded's ps5-native-app-boilerplate
(GPL-3.0-or-later): its platform layer, Vulkan screen and update check. Its installs are Nightshade's Settings > Apps
(https://github.com/Oliv3rmoon/Nightshade, GPL-3.0-or-later).

Built into eboot.bin:

The self-update kit (kit/): BlackBearReloaded's ps5-native-app-boilerplate (GPL-3.0-or-later), with Nightshade's
  additions (GPL-3.0-or-later)
From PacBrew (https://github.com/ps5-payload-dev/pacbrew-repo), each under its own licence:
  SDL 2 (zlib licence), libcurl (curl licence), OpenSSL (Apache-2.0), zlib (zlib licence),
  zstd (BSD-3-Clause), libpsl (MIT; its Public Suffix List data MPL-2.0).
RADV, Mesa's Vulkan driver for AMD GPUs (PS5 port by Mihawk-99) - MIT License - https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa
Frame interpolation (PS5SX2 by Swordpdf, AMD FidelityFX SDK) - GPL-3.0-or-later / MIT
The shaders the Vulkan screen carries (CRT Royale, CRT Lottes, CRT Pi, Newpixie, Hyllian NTSC, Mattias, NTSC,
  LCD3x, zfast LCD, SameBoy's Game Boy LCD, FSR 1) - each under its own licence: THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in the
  source
Fonts in assets/fonts - SIL Open Font License 1.1 (notices beside them): Inter, Noto Sans JP (a subset),
  IBM Plex Mono; Tabler Icons - MIT License
dlmalloc (Doug Lea) - public domain (CC0)
miniz - MIT License - LICENSE-miniz.txt - https://github.com/richgel999/miniz
QR Code generator 1.8.0 (Project Nayuki) - MIT License - LICENSE-qrcodegen.txt -
  https://github.com/nayuki/QR-Code-generator
stb_image, stb_truetype (Sean Barrett) - public domain / MIT
dr_mp3 (David Reid, based on lieff's minimp3) - public domain or MIT-0 - https://github.com/mackron/dr_libs
stb_vorbis 1.22 (Sean Barrett), with a small patch against damaged headers - public domain or MIT -
  https://github.com/nothings/stb (both decode the background music; the patch is third_party/stb_vorbis/)

Beside eboot.bin:

self-updater.elf, the install helper: the boilerplate's self-update helper (GPL-3.0-or-later) with Nightshade's
  "install" (GPL-3.0-or-later); miniz (MIT) inside it
app-launch.elf, the launch payload: Nightshade's (GPL-3.0-or-later), based on the Game Forwarder's launch payload
  of Mihawk's PS5 RetroArch (https://github.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch, GPL-3.0-or-later)
Lapy helper - MIT License - Lapy-MIT.txt
The selection music (sce_sys/snd0.at9, "Night Drive") - BlackBearReloaded, GPL-3.0-or-later

(https://github.com/Oliv3rmoon/Solanum-Catalog) names for it. No game, key or other copyrighted game data is
